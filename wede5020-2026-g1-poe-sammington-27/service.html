At Precision Tailors &Alterations, we provide professional tailoring and alteration services for students, professional and members of the local community. Whether you need a simple adjustments or a more detailred alteration,we help ensure your clothing fits comfortably and look it's best.

Trouser Alterations

We alter trousers to achieve a comfortable and flattering fit. Services included adjusting the length, waist and overall fit of trousers, making them suitable for everyday wear, school, work or specail occasions.

Suit Alterations

Our suit alteration service helps customers achieve a more polished and comfortable fit. We can adjust suit trousers And jackets to better suit the customer's measurements and preferences.

Dress Alterations

We alter dresses to improve their fit, including adjustments to length,waist and other areas where required. This service is suitable for everyday clothing as well as special occasions.

Shirt Alterations

We provide adjustments to shirts to improve their fir and comfort,including sleeve and skilled adjustments where appropriate.

Clothing Repairs

We repair common clothing issues such as loose seams, damaged stitching,missing buttons and minor tears, helping customers extend the life of their favourite garments.

Custom Tailoring

For customers looking for a more personalised fit,we offer tailoring based on individual measurements and requirements
