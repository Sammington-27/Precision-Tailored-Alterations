Precision Tailored Alterations was established in 2021. The business specialises in clothing alterations,custom tailoring,garment repairs, and fit adjustments for individuals who require to provide customers with affordable, high-quality tailoring solutions delivered with skilled craftsmanship and attention to detail. As demand grows for accessible, convenient services, a professional website will allow the business to reach a wider customer base, showcase it's work and make booking and enquires simple for clients.

Vision Statement

To become a trusted and recognisble name in tailoring and alteration services, known for quality workmanship and building a loyal customers base.
